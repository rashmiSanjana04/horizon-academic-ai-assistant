# Horizon Campus — Attendance Policy

## 1. Minimum Attendance Requirement
1.1. Students are required to maintain a minimum of **80% attendance** across all scheduled lectures, tutorials, and practical sessions for each module.
1.2. Attendance is recorded at every session via sign-in sheets, ID card scanning, or the online attendance system, depending on the venue.

## 2. Consequences of Low Attendance
2.1. Students whose attendance falls **below 80%** for a module will receive a formal **Attendance Warning Letter** from the Student Affairs Office.
2.2. Students whose attendance falls **below 60%** may be barred from sitting the final examination for that module, pending review.
2.3. Repeated attendance issues across multiple modules may trigger a formal academic review meeting with the student's Academic Advisor.

## 3. Authorized Absences
3.1. Absences due to illness, family emergencies, religious observance, or approved university representation (e.g., sports, competitions) are considered **authorized** if supported by valid documentation submitted within **3 working days** of the absence.
3.2. Authorized absences do not count against the 80% threshold, provided documentation is submitted and approved.

## 4. Reporting an Absence
4.1. Students must notify their module coordinator or the Student Affairs Office **before** the missed session where possible, or as soon as reasonably practicable afterward.
4.2. Medical certificates must be from a registered medical practitioner and clearly state the dates the student was unfit to attend.

## 5. Attendance Monitoring
5.1. Attendance is reviewed on a **fortnightly basis** by module coordinators.
5.2. Students can check their current attendance percentage through the Student Portal at any time.

## 6. Appeals on Attendance Decisions
6.1. Students who believe their attendance has been recorded incorrectly, or who wish to appeal an examination bar due to attendance, may submit a written request to the Student Affairs Office within **5 working days** of being notified, along with supporting evidence.
