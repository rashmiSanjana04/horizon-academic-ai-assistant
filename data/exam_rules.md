# Horizon Campus — Examination Rules and Regulations

## 1. General Conduct
1.1. Students must arrive at the examination hall at least **15 minutes** before the scheduled start time.
1.2. Students arriving **more than 30 minutes late** will not be permitted to enter the examination hall.
1.3. No student will be allowed to leave the examination hall during the **first 30 minutes** or the **last 15 minutes** of the exam.
1.4. Valid student ID must be presented and placed on the desk throughout the examination.

## 2. Permitted and Prohibited Items
2.1. Only items specified on the exam paper (e.g., calculators, formula sheets) are permitted.
2.2. Mobile phones, smartwatches, and any electronic communication devices are strictly prohibited and must be switched off and stored in bags at the front of the hall.
2.3. Bags, books, and notes must be left at the designated area, not at the student's desk, unless the exam is open-book.

## 3. Academic Misconduct
3.1. Any form of cheating, including copying, unauthorized communication, or possession of unauthorized material, will result in immediate disqualification from the exam.
3.2. Suspected misconduct will be reported to the Examinations Board and may lead to disciplinary action, including suspension or expulsion.
3.3. Impersonation (sitting an exam for another student) is treated as serious academic fraud and may result in expulsion for both parties involved.

## 4. Special Circumstances
4.1. Students with a documented medical condition or disability may apply for **special examination arrangements** (e.g., extra time, separate room) at least **2 weeks** before the exam period, through the Student Affairs Office.
4.2. Students who fall ill during an exam should notify an invigilator immediately; a medical certificate must be submitted within **3 working days** to be considered for a deferred exam.

## 5. Missed Examinations
5.1. Students who miss an exam due to a valid, documented reason (medical emergency, bereavement, etc.) may apply for a **deferred examination** within 5 working days of the missed exam.
5.2. Missing an exam without a valid, approved reason will result in a mark of **zero** for that examination.

## 6. Results and Grading
6.1. Provisional results are typically released within **4 weeks** of the examination date.
6.2. Students who believe there has been an error in marking may request a **remark** within 10 working days of results being published (see Appeals Policy for the formal process).
