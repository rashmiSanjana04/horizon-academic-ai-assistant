# Horizon Campus — Late Submission Policy

## 1. Standard Deadline Policy
1.1. All coursework, assignments, and projects must be submitted by the deadline specified on the assignment brief and course portal.
1.2. Submission timestamps are recorded automatically by the online submission system (e.g., LMS/Moodle) and are considered final.

## 2. Late Penalty Structure
2.1. Work submitted **up to 24 hours** after the deadline will be penalized by **10%** of the total marks available.
2.2. Work submitted **between 24 and 48 hours** late will be penalized by **20%** of the total marks available.
2.3. Work submitted **more than 48 hours (2 days)** after the deadline will **not be accepted** and will be recorded as a **non-submission (0 marks)**, unless an extension has been granted.

## 3. Extensions
3.1. Students may request an extension **before the deadline** by submitting an Extension Request Form to the relevant module coordinator, along with supporting evidence (medical certificate, family emergency documentation, etc.).
3.2. Extensions of up to **5 working days** may be granted by the module coordinator. Longer extensions require approval from the Head of Department.
3.3. Extension requests made **after** the deadline has passed will generally not be considered, except in cases of documented emergencies (see Section 4).

## 4. Mitigating Circumstances
4.1. If a student is unable to submit on time due to circumstances beyond their control (serious illness, bereavement, accident), they may submit a **Mitigating Circumstances Claim** within 5 working days of the deadline.
4.2. Approved mitigating circumstances claims may result in a **penalty-free extension** or the opportunity to submit as a **first attempt** at a later date.

## 5. Group Assignments
5.1. For group submissions, late penalties apply equally to all group members unless individual mitigating circumstances are documented and approved.

## 6. Technical Issues
6.1. System outages on the submission portal that are confirmed by IT Services will not be penalized; affected students should email their lecturer immediately with a screenshot of the error.
6.2. "My internet was down" or "my laptop crashed" without third-party evidence is **not** considered a valid reason for a penalty-free late submission.
