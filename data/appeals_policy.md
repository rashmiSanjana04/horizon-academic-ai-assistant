# Horizon Campus — Academic Appeals Policy

## 1. Grounds for Appeal
An academic appeal may be submitted on one or more of the following grounds:
1.1. **Procedural irregularity** — the assessment or examination process was not conducted in line with published regulations.
1.2. **New evidence** — relevant evidence (e.g., medical documentation) was not available at the time of assessment through no fault of the student.
1.3. **Bias or improper conduct** — evidence that an assessor acted with bias or that the assessment was conducted improperly.

> Note: Disagreement with academic judgment alone (i.e., simply believing you deserved a higher grade) is **not** valid grounds for appeal.

## 2. Appeal Process — Step by Step
2.1. **Stage 1 — Informal Resolution:** Students should first discuss the concern directly with the module lecturer or academic advisor within **5 working days** of receiving the result.
2.2. **Stage 2 — Formal Appeal:** If unresolved, students must submit a **Formal Appeal Form** to the Examinations Office within **10 working days** of the official result release, along with supporting evidence.
2.3. **Stage 3 — Academic Appeals Committee Review:** The committee reviews the appeal and evidence, and may request a meeting with the student. A decision is typically issued within **15 working days**.
2.4. **Stage 4 — Final Review:** If the student is dissatisfied with the committee's decision, a final review may be requested from the Vice Chancellor's Office within 5 working days of the decision. This is the final internal stage of appeal.

## 3. Required Documentation
3.1. A completed Formal Appeal Form.
3.2. A written statement outlining the grounds for appeal.
3.3. Any supporting evidence (medical certificates, email correspondence, incident reports, etc.).

## 4. Outcomes
An appeal may result in one of the following outcomes:
4.1. **Appeal upheld** — the grade/decision may be amended, or the student may be offered a re-assessment.
4.2. **Appeal partially upheld** — specific remedial action is taken (e.g., a re-mark) without full reversal of the original decision.
4.3. **Appeal rejected** — the original decision stands, with reasons provided in writing.

## 5. Important Notes
5.1. Submitting an appeal does **not** guarantee a change in outcome.
5.2. Appeals must be submitted in good faith; frivolous or vexatious appeals may be dismissed without full review.
5.3. While an appeal is under review, the original grade remains on the student's record provisionally.
